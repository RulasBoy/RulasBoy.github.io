About me

I'm a data analyst and software developer from Mexico who works end-to-end: I take raw, messy data — call logs, exports, spreadsheets — and turn it into clean analysis, dashboards, and forecasts teams use to make decisions.

My background in backend and DevOps means I understand data from its source, not just once it's tidy. I've built production systems that forecast demand and automate reporting.

What I care about most is the "so what" — a chart nobody acts on is just decoration.
